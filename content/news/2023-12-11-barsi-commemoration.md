---
title: "MBSCET Commemorates Barsi of Srimaan Mahant Bachittar Singh Ji"
date: 2023-12-11
category: "Campus"
---
MBSCET organized a commemoration of the Barsi (death anniversary) of Srimaan Mahant Bachittar Singh Ji, the founder patron of the institution. The event was attended by members of the Sant Manjit Singh Trust, college management, faculty, and students. Floral tributes were offered and speeches highlighted the visionary legacy of Mahant Bachittar Singh Ji in establishing the college for the benefit of the community.
