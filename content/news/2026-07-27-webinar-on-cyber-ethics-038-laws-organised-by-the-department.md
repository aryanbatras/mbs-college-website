Department of Computer Science & Engineering, MBSCET organised a webinar on Cyber Ethics & Laws on 29th of September 2022 in virtual mode. The students of  CSE Department along with the faculty members of the department participated in the session. Dr. Sheeba Singh, Director FINNLOGIC-IT Services Pvt LTD. Bangalore was the Guest Speaker who organised a very interactive & informative session. The webinar was co-ordinated by Ms. Shalini Sharma, & Ms. Jasmeen Kaur.
