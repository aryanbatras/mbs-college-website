Jammu, June 19, 2026: The Training and Placement Cell of Mahant Bachittar Singh College of Engineering & Technology (MBSCET), Jammu, organized a campus placement drive in collaboration with Kandhari Beverages Pvt. Ltd., Samba, for students of Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, and Electronics & Communication Engineering belonging to the batches of 2021, 2022, and 2023.

The recruitment drive witnessed participation from eligible candidates seeking employment opportunities in the manufacturing and beverage industry. Following a comprehensive selection process comprising interviews and candidate assessments,  marking a noteworthy achievement for the institution and the participating students.

The company offered attractive career opportunities along with competitive remuneration and promising growth prospects, providing students with a valuable platform to begin their professional careers.

The Management, Principal, faculty members, and the Training and Placement Cell of MBSCET expressed their sincere gratitude to the recruitment team of Kandhari Beverages Pvt. Ltd. for conducting the campus recruitment drive and providing employment opportunities to the students.

A special note of appreciation was extended to Mr. Abhinav Kumar, HR Head, Kandhari Beverages Pvt. Ltd., and Mr. Ashish, Recruitment Executive, for their support, cooperation, and professional engagement throughout the recruitment process. The college also thanked the entire recruitment team for their valuable time, guidance, and efforts in identifying talented candidates from the institution.

Speaking on the occasion, the college administration reiterated its commitment to strengthening industry-academia partnerships and creating meaningful employment opportunities for students. The placement drive reflects the institution&#8217;s continuous efforts towards enhancing employability and career development among its students.

The selected students expressed their gratitude to the company and the Training and Placement Cell for providing them with an opportunity to showcase their skills and secure employment.

MBSCET remains dedicated to fostering strong industrial collaborations and facilitating quality placement opportunities for its students, thereby contributing to their professional growth and career advancement.
