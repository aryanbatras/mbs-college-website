title: "MBS College Organized Two-Day Workshop on Power BI"
date: 2026-04-09
category: "Workshop"

MBS College of Engineering and Technology organized a two-day workshop on Power BI for Data Analytics and Business Intelligence. The workshop was conducted by the Department of Information Technology and covered data visualization, dashboard creation, and business intelligence fundamentals. Students gained hands-on experience with Power BI tools and learned to analyze real-world datasets for decision-making.
