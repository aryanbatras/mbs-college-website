On 19th May, 2026,  Department of Information Technology organized a workshop on Power BI for Data Analytics and Business Intelligence for 4th Semester students of the IT Department. Ms. Pavneet Kour (Lead Expert Data Analyst) and Mr. Shubham Jasrotia (Senior Executive Data Scientist), Winnovation Education Services Pvt. Ltd., Jammu were the speakers who provided  hands-on session to create  interactive visualizations and dashboards using real datasets to the students.
